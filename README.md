# ConceptDetective-AI


### See what you understand. Discover what you're missing.

Concept Detective is an EdTech web application that helps students understand their conceptual gaps instead of simply telling them whether an answer is right or wrong.

## 💡 What It Does

The platform asks concept-based questions according to the student's education level and subject. It analyzes the student's answer and reasoning to identify:

- What the student understands
- Possible misconceptions
- Missing concepts
- Conceptual clarity
- Suggestions for improvement

The **TeachBack** feature then asks students to explain the concept in their own words, helping them reinforce genuine understanding.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Select your education level
2. Select a subject
3. Receive a conceptual question
4. Submit your answer and reasoning
5. Analyze your conceptual understanding
6. Identify misconceptions and missing concepts
7. Use TeachBack to explain the concept in your own words
8. Receive a final learning report

## 🚀 Key Innovation

Traditional learning systems often focus on whether an answer is correct.

**Concept Detective focuses on WHY the student thinks that way.**

It aims to detect the misconception behind the mistake and guide the learner toward better conceptual understanding.
